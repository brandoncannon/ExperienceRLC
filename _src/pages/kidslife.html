Commonly Asked Questions

For parents

What if it's our first time at Kids Life?

At Kids Life Check-In, our hosts will have you fill out a short form about your child (or children) and give you a Security Tag. If we need to reach you during the service, we'll send a text message to your phone. Present your Security Tag at the end of service to pick up your child. For safety, no child will be released without the correct Security Tag.

Can I come to Kids Life with my child?

You're welcome to peek in and see how your child is doing. To be in a classroom, we require volunteers to have a background check and interview with us, because the safety of your child is of the utmost importance. If you'd like to serve in a class, we'd love to have you on the team! Email us to find out more.

What will my child do?

Your child will have a blast! Every classroom has activities and toys designed for your child's age. They'll experience praise and worship, a creatively communicated Bible lesson, crafts, games, and more. We have fun serving God, and it's contagious!

What class does my child go to?

Kids Life has three main groups: Little Life (birth – 1 year), Kids Life Jr. (2 years – preschool), and Kids Life (K – 5th grade). Have other questions? Email us.

How can I lead my child to Christ?

One of our chief goals is to equip Kids Life parents to lead their children to know God from an early age. When your child begins asking faith questions, God may be opening their heart to the Gospel. View our parent guide so you're ready when that moment comes.
