This is not a crisis center. If you are experiencing an emergency, please call 911 or the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline at 988. Your life matters. Jesus loves you.

Life can be painful and confusing, and there are times in life's journey when we need one-on-one soul care and spiritual direction.

At RLCC, our desire is for you to experience a heart change through the truth of Scripture. Through Biblical counseling, we apply Scripture to the challenges we all face as a result of living in a sinful, fallen world.

We offer up to 16 weeks of free Biblical counseling to members and non-members alike. If you need counseling, please fill out the intake form and one of our counselors will reach out to you as soon as possible.
