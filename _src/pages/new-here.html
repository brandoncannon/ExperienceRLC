Common Questions

Before you come

What should I wear?

There is no dress code at Real Life Church. Some people dress casually and some dress up, and it's all good! You are free to come exactly as you are, so wear whatever you're most comfortable in.

What is service like?

We always start with a time of energetic, passionate praise and worship. From there, you'll hear a Bible-centered message from Pastor Brandon or someone from our preaching team, followed by a time to respond to God's Word. We end by sharing a few announcements about what's happening at RLC and praying over the congregation.

What happens when I arrive?

You'll enter the lobby and be greeted with a friendly smile from one of our greeters. There's coffee for you to enjoy on us. Let our greeting team know if you need to check your kids into KidsLife. A 5-minute countdown lets you know when service will start, so you can grab your seat before it begins.

What about my kids?

Our KidsLife ministry provides an amazing experience at every service. Visit the KidsLife check-in kiosk, where our team will register your children and give you a parent pick-up tag. Please let us know about any allergies or special requirements during check-in.

At the end of the service, show your parent pick-up tag to the security team at the KidsLife entrance, then to a teacher in your child's classroom, and your child will be released to you.

How can I meet others and get connected?

The best way is RLC Connect, a one-time event with one goal: to give you what you need to connect with our church community and take next steps in your relationship with Jesus. You'll hear the story of RLC, our vision, meet the team, and learn how to get connected. Dinner is free and childcare is provided.
