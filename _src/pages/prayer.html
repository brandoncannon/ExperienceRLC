Fasting disconnects us from the world

Fasting

Fasting is one of the least known disciplines in the church, but it's mentioned in both the Old and New Testaments and by Jesus Himself. It isn't about punishing yourself; it's about intentionally disconnecting from worldly things for a season so you can hear from God and experience the full life He has for you.

Our History

Real Life Church has been built on prayer.

It was during a season of prayer and fasting in 2010 that God gave Brandon Cannon the vision for planting a life-giving church. Because our church came from a season of prayer and fasting, it has always been a priority.

Although January is the best time of year to start a church, Pastor Brandon and the team postponed the launch of RLC until February 2012 in order to devote the first of the year to prayer and fasting. From that January until now, Real Life Church has spent 21 days every January and August in prayer, and it is one of the most important things we do as a church.
