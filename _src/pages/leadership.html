Our Leadership

Leading with integrity.

Believers who invest their hearts, time, families, and finances in building a local church deserve to have confidence in its leadership.

01 · We are guided by pastors

Our Team

The pastoral staff team is led by the Senior Pastor and oversees the day-to-day ministry and operations of the church. These staff pastors serve the congregation and are responsible for developing its spiritual life. The Senior Pastor Leadership Team members serve as Staff Elders.

02 · We are protected by trustees

Trustees

The Trustees are members of the congregation who oversee the finances and direct the provision of the facilities needed by the church. They provide counsel to the Senior Pastor Leadership Team on major financial commitments, and serve as Non-staff Elders.

03 · We are strengthened by oversight

Pastoral Oversight Committee

The Overseers are pastors of respected congregations and ministries who love Real Life Church and provide spiritual protection to it. They may be called in to help with accountability matters relating to the Senior Pastor if requested by the Pastors or Trustees, and serve as Apostolic Elders.
