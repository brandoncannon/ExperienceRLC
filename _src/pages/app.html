Get the App

Real Life Church on your phone

Daily Reading & SOAP, Message Notes, the Worship Guide, giving and more, one tap from your phone's home screen. It's free.

There are two ways to get the app. Pick whichever you like.

Option 1

Download it from the app store

Get the Real Life Church app from the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android).

Get the RLC App
Option 2

Add the website to your home screen

No download needed. It puts an RLC icon on your phone that opens the website full-screen, like an app.

You're all set

You're using the RLC app. It works just like the website: tap ☰ at the top for the menu.

On iPhone

  1. 1
    Open experiencerlc.com in Safari.
  2. 2
    Tap the Share button {{share_icon}} at the bottom of the screen.
  3. 3
    Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. 4
    Tap Add. The RLC icon appears on your home screen.

On Android

  1. 1
    Open experiencerlc.com in Chrome.
  2. 2
    Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  3. 3
    Choose Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  4. 4
    Tap Install. The RLC icon appears with your other apps.

Always up to date

It's the RLC website, so new SOAPs, message notes and events show up automatically. No updates to install.

Works on a weak signal

Pages you've opened before are saved, so they still load when your connection drops.
