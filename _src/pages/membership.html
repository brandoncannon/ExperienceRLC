Part 1

Why it matters

Church membership starts with something bigger than a church. Before you can belong to a church family, you first need to belong to Jesus.

Part 1 video coming soon

The Gospel: the good news about Jesus

  1. God loves you and made you for a relationship with Him.You are not an accident. God created you in His image, and He loves you more than you can imagine.Genesis 1:27; John 3:16
  2. Sin separates us from God.Every one of us has gone our own way instead of God's way. The result of sin is death: separation from God, now and forever. No amount of good works can fix it.Romans 3:23; Romans 6:23; Isaiah 59:2
  3. Jesus paid the price for our sin.Jesus, God the Son, lived a sinless life, died on the cross in our place, was buried, and rose from the dead on the third day. He did for us what we could never do for ourselves.Romans 5:8; 1 Corinthians 15:3–4; 1 Peter 3:18
  4. Salvation is a gift you receive by faith.You can't earn it. You receive it by turning from your sin (repentance) and putting your trust in Jesus as your Savior and Lord.Ephesians 2:8–9; Romans 10:9–10; Acts 3:19
  5. New life begins, and you are not alone.When you trust Jesus, you are forgiven, made new, and adopted into God's family. Baptism is how you go public with that decision, and the church is the family you grow in.2 Corinthians 5:17; John 1:12; Acts 2:38–41

Ready to trust Jesus?

You can talk to God right now, in your own words. Something like this:

Jesus, I know I have sinned and I need You. I believe You died for my sins and rose from the dead. I turn from my sin and put my trust in You. Be my Savior and Lord. Thank You for forgiving me and giving me new life. Amen.

If you prayed that for the first time, we want to celebrate with you and help you take your next step. Let us know here, talk to any pastor, or see Water Baptism.

Why church membership matters

You belong to a family, not just a crowd.

When you follow Jesus, you become part of His body. God never meant for you to follow Him alone. Membership is how you say, "This is my church family."

Romans 12:5; 1 Corinthians 12:27; Ephesians 2:19

The early church knew who belonged.

Those who believed were baptized and added to the church, and they devoted themselves to one another. People were counted, known, and cared for.

Acts 2:41–47

Pastors are called to shepherd specific people.

Scripture asks leaders to care for the flock "under their care" and to give an account for it. Membership tells us who we are responsible to love, pray for, and walk with.

Hebrews 13:17; 1 Peter 5:2–3

Commitment helps you grow.

You grow most where you are known. Membership moves you from attending to belonging, and from spiritual consumer to spiritual contributor.

Hebrews 10:24–25; Ephesians 4:15–16

Reflect: Have you trusted Jesus for your salvation? If so, where are you on the journey from attending a church to belonging to one?

Our purpose

Everyone matters to God. We believe life is simply better when we live it on mission with Jesus Christ.

Our mission

Make disciples the Jesus way by knowing God, growing in freedom, and sharing Christ in everyday life.

Matthew 28:19–20; Matthew 4:19

Our five values, and how members live them

  • We love God.Daily time in the Word and prayer, and a public yes to Jesus in baptism. Lived out in: Testimony
  • We see people.Real community in a Life Group, and noticing the people around you who need Jesus and a friend. Lived out in: Unity and Responsibility
  • We make it better.Using your gifts to serve, and getting equipped so what you do keeps improving. Lived out in: Ministry
  • We choose joy.Regular gratitude, rest, and celebration, even when circumstances are hard. Lived out in: Testimony
  • We live to give.Generous giving of your money, your time, and your life so others can follow Jesus. Lived out in: Responsibility and Testimony

Part 3

What it means to be a member

Being a member means you are saying, "With God's help, I will..."

Part 3 video coming soon
1

Unity · How I relate to my church

Protect the unity of my church.

  • Demonstrate love toward fellow members.
  • Refuse to participate in gossip.
  • Follow church leadership.

Following leadership means trusting and supporting leaders who are themselves accountable to God and His Word. It isn't blind obedience. See how RLC is led in Part 4.

Romans 14:19; Romans 15:5; 1 Peter 1:22; Ephesians 4:29; Hebrews 13:17
2

Responsibility · How I take ownership of my church

Share the responsibility of my church.

  • Pray for the church's health and growth.
  • Invite people who do not attend church.
  • Make guests feel welcome.
1 Thessalonians 1:1–2; Luke 14:23; Romans 15:7
3

Ministry · How I serve through my church

Serve the ministry of my church.

  • Discover my spiritual gifts and abilities.
  • Use those gifts to make a difference in others' lives.
  • Develop the heart of a servant.

Start with the Connecting the Dots assessments (ask a pastor or your Life Group leader), then try a Dream Team.

1 Peter 4:10; Ephesians 4:11–12; Philippians 2:4–7
4

Testimony · How I represent my church

Support the testimony of my church.

  • Faithfully participate in worship services and Life Groups.
  • Live a life that honors God.
  • Practice consistent financial giving.
Acts 2:42; Hebrews 10:25; Philippians 1:27; 2 Corinthians 9:7

Before you join

Membership at Real Life Church is for people who have:

  • Trusted Jesus as Savior and Lord (Part 1).
  • Been baptized as a believer. Not yet? Sign up for Water Baptism, and you can take both steps together.
  • Completed this course and are ready to make the four commitments.
What we believe, in brief
  • The Bible is God's inspired Word and our final authority.
  • There is one God, eternally existing as Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.
  • Jesus Christ is fully God and fully man. He lived a sinless life, died for our sins, rose again, and will return.
  • We are saved by grace through faith in Jesus, not by our own works.
  • The Holy Spirit gives new life and empowers believers to live for God.

Read our full statement of beliefs

How RLC is led
  • Guided by pastors. The pastoral staff, led by the Senior Pastor, oversees day-to-day ministry. The Senior Pastor Leadership Team serves as Staff Elders.
  • Protected by trustees. Trustees are church members who oversee finances and facilities, counsel the pastors on major financial commitments, and serve as Non-staff Elders.
  • Strengthened by oversight. Pastors of respected congregations serve as Apostolic Elders and help with accountability for the Senior Pastor when needed.

Meet our leadership

Our commitment to you

Membership goes both ways. As your church, we commit to:

  • Teach God's Word faithfully.
  • Pray for you and care for you, especially in hard seasons.
  • Lead with integrity and be open about our direction and finances.
  • Help you find your next step and your place to serve.
  • Equip you to make disciples of your own.
When there's conflict, or when life changes

Families don't always agree. When there's a conflict, we go directly to the person, humbly and privately, with the goal of restoration, and bring in a pastor if needed (Matthew 18:15–17; Galatians 6:1).

If you move away or feel God leading you elsewhere, please talk to a pastor. We'd love to bless you and send you well instead of losing you quietly.

After you join

Membership isn't the finish line. It's a starting line. Your next steps:

  • Join a Life Group
  • Take the Connecting the Dots assessments
  • Serve on a Dream Team
  • Give regularly
  • Invite one person to a service or Life Group

See the full path on Next Steps.
