The thief's purpose is to steal and kill and destroy. My purpose is to give them a rich and satisfying life.John 10:10

We want to see middle and high school students thriving in an authentic relationship with God and connected to the local church.

Our goal is to accept, believe in, and challenge every student so they can be part of the Body of Christ. We do that by helping them Follow Jesus, Grow in Freedom, and Share Christ with others. As students take these steps, they grow closer to God, build great relationships, and learn to serve and lead in the church.

When we meet

Every Wednesday night at 6:30pm. We're excited to welcome you and get to know you!

What to expect

A safe environment where students are accepted for who they are and challenged by each other, and from the Bible, to be leaders of this generation.

Who it's for

Student Life begins in 6th grade and continues through high school.
