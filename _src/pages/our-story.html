Our Story

God is at work at Real Life Church.

Pastor Brandon Cannon dreamed of planting a church with a simple goal: help people connect with God in a church where they felt loved, valued, and part of something bigger than themselves.

With the help of the Dream Team, 34 people committed to seeing this dream become a reality, Real Life Church began on February 5, 2012. That first Sunday, almost 200 people attended at the Great Escapes Movie Theater.

Pastor Brandon focused on reaching out to the non-churched community and building relationships through life groups meeting throughout the city. The life-giving message of the Gospel, the passion and joy of Sunday Celebrations, and the simple style of Real Life Church connected with people in an amazing way. In its first year, the church grew and over 100 people committed their lives to Christ. At the same time, the church helped plant other churches through an organization called the ARC.
