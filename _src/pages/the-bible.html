The SOAP method

Being devoted to Jesus is a life-long journey, and we're committed to helping you along the way. We encourage you to use the SOAP daily devotional process, and to write your thoughts in a journal as you go. Journaling is an excellent way to record and process what God speaks to you.

S

Scripture

Take your time reading each chapter and allow God to speak to you. Look for a verse that particularly spoke to you and write it in your journal.

O

Observation

What do you think God is saying to you in this scripture? Ask the Holy Spirit to teach you. Paraphrase the scripture in your own words.

A

Application

Personalize what you've read by asking how it applies to your life right now. Write how this scripture can apply to you today.

P

Prayer

Ask God to help you use this scripture, or pray over what He's revealing. Prayer is a two-way conversation, so listen and journal what He says.
