The greatest story ever told is the [mission] of God to [rescue all of us].
Season Two: Our God came to [our rescue].
(Romans 1:16-17) For I am not ashamed of this Good News about Christ. It is the power of God at work… This Good News tells us how God makes us right in his sight. This is accomplished from start to finish by faith. As the Scriptures say, “It is through faith that a righteous person has life.”
(Ephesians 4:17b-24) Live no longer as the Gentiles do, for they are hopelessly confused. Their minds are full of darkness; they wander far from the life God gives because they have closed their minds and hardened their hearts against him. They have no sense of shame. They live for lustful pleasure and eagerly practice every kind of impurity. But that isn’t what you learned about Christ. Since you have heard about Jesus and have learned the truth that comes from him, throw off your old sinful nature and your former way of life, which is corrupted by lust and deception. Instead, let the Spirit renew your thoughts and attitudes. Put on your new nature, created to be like God—truly righteous and holy.
Principle: The Gospel is the power of God that [transforms] lives.
In this life, we will face at least three enemies:
The [world], the [flesh], and the [devil].
(Philippians 4:6-7) Don’t worry about anything; instead, pray about everything. Tell God what you need, and thank him for all he has done. Then you will experience God’s peace, which exceeds anything we can understand. His peace will guard your hearts and minds as you live in Christ Jesus.
### The practical result of receiving the Gospel is now…

# (v6a) Through [prayer], we can know the peace that comes through [fellowship with God].
(1 John 5:20) We know that the Son of God has come, and he has given us understanding so that we can know the true God. And now we live in fellowship with the true God because we live in fellowship with his Son, Jesus Christ. He is the only true God, and he is eternal life.
(Matthew 6:9-13) Pray like this: Our Father in heaven, may your name be kept holy. May your Kingdom come soon. May your will be done on earth, as it is in heaven. Give us today the food we need, and forgive us our sins, as we have forgiven those who sin against us. And don’t let us yield to temptation, but rescue us from the evil one.
Takeaway: The Gospel gives us the ability to [make room] for God.

# (v6b) Through [asking], we can know the peace that comes with getting [God involved].
(1 Peter 5:7) Give all your worries and cares to God, for he cares about you.
(Acts 12:1-6, 12-14, 16) About that time King Herod Agrippa began to persecute some believers in the church. He had the apostle James killed with a sword… he also arrested Peter… Herod intended to bring Peter out for public trial… But while Peter was in prison, the church prayed very earnestly for him. The night before Peter was to be placed on trial, he was asleep… Peter finally came to his senses. “It really is true.”… When he realized this, he went to the home… where many were gathered for prayer. He knocked at the door in the gate, and a servant girl named Rhoda came to open it. When she recognized Peter’s voice, she was so overjoyed that, instead of opening the door, she ran back inside and told everyone, “Peter is standing at the door!”… Meanwhile, Peter continued knocking. When they finally opened the door and saw him, they were amazed.
Takeaway: The Gospel gives us the ability to [bring it all] to God.

# (v6c) Through [thankfulness], we can know the peace that [defeats worry].
(Psalm 103:1-5) Let all that I am praise the Lord; may I never forget the good things he does for me. He forgives all my sins and heals all my diseases. He redeems me from death and crowns me with love and tender mercies. He fills my life with good things. My youth is renewed like the eagle’s!
Takeaway: The Gospel gives us the ability to [focus] on God.
(Romans 8:38-39) I am convinced that [nothing] can ever separate us from God’s love. Neither death nor life, neither angels nor demons, neither our fears for today nor our worries about tomorrow—not even the powers of hell can separate us from God’s love. No power in the sky above or in the earth below—indeed, nothing in all creation will ever be able to separate us from the love of God that is revealed in Christ Jesus our Lord.
