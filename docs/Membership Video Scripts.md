# Membership Course: Video Scripts

Four videos, one for each part of the course at `/membership/`. Aim for 5–8 minutes each. Every video follows the same shape: **hook → teaching → key verse on screen → reflection question → tease the next part.** These are talking points written to be spoken. Put them in your own words.

---

## Video 1: Why It Matters (the Gospel and why the church)

**On camera:** Pastor Brandon

**Hook (30 sec)**
"Think about the difference between staying at a hotel and living in a home. At a hotel, you're a guest. You use what's there, and you leave. At home, you belong. You have a key, a room, and people who notice when you're gone. A lot of people treat church like a hotel. God designed it to be a home. But before we talk about belonging to a church, we have to talk about belonging to Jesus."

**The Gospel (3 min)**
1. *God loves you and made you for a relationship with Him.* You're not an accident. (Genesis 1:27; John 3:16)
2. *Sin separates us from God.* All of us have gone our own way. The result is death, separation from God, and no amount of being good can fix it. (Romans 3:23; 6:23)
3. *Jesus paid the price.* He lived the life we couldn't, died the death we deserved, and rose again on the third day. (Romans 5:8; 1 Corinthians 15:3–4)
4. *Salvation is a gift you receive by faith.* You can't earn it. You turn from your sin and trust Jesus as Savior and Lord. (Ephesians 2:8–9; Romans 10:9–10)
5. *New life begins, and you're not alone.* You're forgiven, made new, and adopted into God's family. Baptism is how you go public. (2 Corinthians 5:17; John 1:12)

**Invitation (1 min)**
"If you've never trusted Jesus, you can do that right now." Lead the prayer slowly (the text is on the page). "If you prayed that for the first time, don't keep it to yourself. Use the link under this video to tell us, because we want to celebrate with you and help you take your next step, starting with baptism."

**Why church membership (2 min)**
- *You belong to a family, not a crowd.* "Each member belongs to all the others." (Romans 12:5)
- *The early church knew who belonged.* They were baptized, added, and devoted to one another. (Acts 2:41–47)
- *Pastors shepherd specific people.* Hebrews 13:17 says leaders "give an account." Membership tells us who we're responsible to care for.
- *Commitment helps you grow.* You grow most where you're known. Membership moves you from consumer to contributor.

**Key verse on screen:** Romans 12:5

**Reflect:** "Have you trusted Jesus? And where are you between attending a church and belonging to one?"

**Tease:** "In Part 2, I'll share what God has called Real Life Church to do, and where you fit in it."

## Video 2: Our Vision, Our Values, and Where You Fit

**On camera:** Pastor Brandon, with photos and video from Sundays, Life Groups, and Dream Teams

**Hook (30 sec)**
Tell a short part of the RLC story: 34 people with a dream, the first Sunday at the Great Escapes theater in 2012, and almost 200 people. "That dream wasn't about a building. It was about people."

**Purpose and mission (1.5 min)**
- Purpose: *Everyone matters to God.*
- Mission: *Make disciples the Jesus way, by knowing God, growing in freedom, and sharing Christ in everyday life.* It's built on Matthew 28:19–20 and Matthew 4:19.
- "We filter every idea, program, and budget request through this. If it doesn't help us make disciples, we don't do it."

**Five values (3 min).** Take about 30 seconds per value and pair each with a real example from the church:
- **We love God:** Word, prayer, baptism.
- **We see people:** Life Groups, and noticing the person who needs Jesus and a friend.
- **We make it better:** serving with excellence and getting equipped.
- **We choose joy:** gratitude, rest, and celebration, even in hard seasons.
- **We live to give:** money, time, and life, so others can follow Jesus.

Mention that the four member commitments in Part 3 are how these values get lived out.

**Where you fit (1 min)**
"We're not a church that *has* Life Groups. We're a church *of* Life Groups." Every member needs three things: a place to **belong** (a Life Group), a place to **serve** (a Dream Team), and a next step to **grow** (Next Steps).

**Key verse on screen:** Matthew 28:19–20

**Reflect:** "Which value comes most naturally to you? Which one stretches you?"

**Tease:** "Next, we'll look at what you're actually saying yes to when you become a member."

## Video 3: What It Means to Be a Member

**On camera:** Pastor Brandon introduces it, then a different member shares a 30–45 second story for each commitment.

**Hook (30 sec)**
"Membership isn't a club card. It's a commitment, and it goes both ways. Here are the four things every member of Real Life Church says, with God's help, 'I will.'"

**1. Unity: how I relate to my church (1.5 min)**
- Love fellow members, refuse to gossip, and follow church leadership.
- Explain clearly: following leadership means trusting leaders who are themselves accountable to God and His Word. It is not blind obedience.
- Gossip line: "If you aren't part of the problem or part of the solution, it's gossip."
- Scripture: Romans 14:19; Ephesians 4:29; Hebrews 13:17
- *Member story:* someone who saw unity protected, or was loved well, during a hard time.

**2. Responsibility: how I take ownership (1.5 min)**
- Pray for the church, invite people who don't go to church, and make guests feel welcome.
- "We don't exist *for* the church; we *are* the church."
- Scripture: Luke 14:23; Romans 15:7
- *Member story:* someone who came because a member invited them.

**3. Ministry: how I serve (1.5 min)**
- Discover your gifts (Connecting the Dots), use them, and develop a servant's heart.
- "You're never closer to the heart of God than when you're serving."
- Scripture: 1 Peter 4:10; Philippians 2:4–7
- *Member story:* a Dream Team member.

**4. Testimony: how I represent my church (1.5 min)**
- Show up for worship and Life Group, live a life that honors God, and give consistently.
- Talk about giving plainly and without pressure: "Generosity is a response to God's generosity." (2 Corinthians 9:7)
- Scripture: Acts 2:42; Hebrews 10:25; Philippians 1:27
- *Member story:* someone whose life or giving changed.

**Key verse on screen:** Philippians 1:27

**Reflect:** "Which commitment are you already living? Which one will take the biggest step of faith?"

**Tease:** "In the last part, I'll show you exactly how to take the step and become a member."

## Video 4: Your Next Step, Becoming a Member

**On camera:** Pastor Brandon

**Hook (30 sec)**
"You've heard why it matters, who we are, and what members commit to. Now let's talk about how you actually make RLC home."

**Before you join (45 sec)**
Membership is for people who have trusted Jesus, been baptized as a believer, and completed this course. "If you haven't been baptized, that's not a roadblock. It's your next step, and we'll help you take it."

**What we believe (1 min)**
Give the short version: the Bible is our final authority; one God as Father, Son, and Holy Spirit; Jesus is fully God and fully man, died and rose again; we are saved by grace through faith. Point to the Beliefs page for the full statement.

**How RLC is led (1 min)**
- Guided by **pastors**: the Senior Pastor Leadership Team serves as Staff Elders.
- Protected by **trustees**: members who oversee finances and serve as Non-staff Elders.
- Strengthened by **oversight**: outside pastors who serve as Apostolic Elders and provide accountability.
- "You deserve to have confidence in the people leading your church."

**Our commitment to you (1 min)**
"Membership goes both ways." We will teach God's Word faithfully, pray for you and care for you, lead with integrity and be open about our finances, help you find your place, and equip you to make disciples.

**Conflict and leaving well (30 sec)**
Matthew 18:15: go to the person directly, with restoration as the goal. "And if God ever moves you somewhere else, talk to us. We'd love to bless you and send you well."

**After you join (30 sec)**
"Membership is a starting line, not a finish line." Join a Life Group, discover your gifts, serve, give, and invite one person.

**The invitation (1 min). End the video on this.**
"Here's how to join:
1. Fill out the membership form right below this video. It takes two minutes.
2. We'll set up a time for you to meet with me or one of our pastors. We want to hear your story and answer your questions.
3. You'll sign the membership commitment.
4. And we'll celebrate you as part of the Real Life Church family.

If you're ready to make RLC home, fill out the form below. I can't wait to meet you."

**End screen:** "Fill out the membership form below," with the button visible on the page.
