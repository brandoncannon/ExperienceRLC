The Dream Team

Serve God by serving His people.

If you desire a life filled with joy, growth, and community, come join the team!

Join the Dream Team
What is the Dream Team?

Incredible people serving in their gifts and passions.

From creating a comfortable environment on a Host Team, to leading the next generation to Jesus in Student Ministries, to crafting a dynamic experience with the Service Experience Team, to tearing down barriers across our city by leading a Life Group, we are a group that forcefully advances the Kingdom of God.

Our mission: to build the body of Christ by each person fulfilling their calling and serving to make an eternal difference.

Serve

Serving is in the DNA of RLC. It started when Pastor Brandon launched with a group of Dream Team members passionate about bringing a dynamic, life-giving church to Bowling Green. As we've grown, our culture has remained one of selfless service to our incredible God and His people.

[Jesus] made Himself nothing, taking the very nature of a servant…Philippians 2:6-8
Care

We want to create a community of care, acceptance, and warmth for every guest, and a community of support, encouragement, and prayer for every team member. We strive to keep a Life Group feel within each team, because for many, their service team is their small group.

They devoted themselves to the apostles' teaching and to the fellowship…Acts 2:42-47
Movement

Our win is when people take steps toward becoming spiritually mature: passionate, relationally connected, and united as the Body of Christ, humbly serving in their passions, advancing the Kingdom, worshiping God with every act of service, and walking in complete fulfillment of their purpose.

And we… are being transformed into His likeness with ever-increasing glory.2 Corinthians 3:18
Areas of Service

Find your team

First Responders

This unit of health care providers triages basic medical attention for our guests in the event of an accident or emergency at RLC.

Greeters

This friendly team loves people and makes them feel at home. They welcome guests with a smile, encouragement, and a personal touch so that hearts are open to the Gospel message.

Hospitality

This energetic team serves a hot cup of coffee and connects with guests through warm conversation and an authentic welcome.

Hope Cafe

This team serves everyone at RLC each week by connecting them with food and missions. All the money made from the cafe supports local, national, and international missions.

Growth Track Team

As the official hosts of Next Steps, this team greets guests and coordinates materials, refreshments, and assistance throughout the event.

Kids Life

This team creates an environment where children are consistently exposed to the real love of Jesus through worship, videos, skits, life groups, games and more.

RLC Worship

With vocals or instruments, this dynamic team prepares the way for our guests to encounter the Presence of the Living God.

Kids Check-In

As families arrive at Kids Life and Preschool/Nursery, this team welcomes them and runs the check-in process that ensures children are cared for well.

Facilities Team

This amazing team cares for the RLC campus through cleaning, maintenance, and maintaining excellence.

Parking Team

The first impression of RLC! If you relish the great outdoors, look good in a uniform, and enjoy telling people where to go, this is the team for you.

Life Group Leader

Real life change takes place in the context of relationships. As a Life Group leader, you can connect with others, develop your gifts, and live out the Great Commission.

Student Ministries

Join the team building the next generation, one dynamic worship experience and relevant Life Group at a time.

Join the Dream Team

Your first step is RLC Connect, where you'll discover your God-given purpose and the team that fits you best.
