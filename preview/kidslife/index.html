KidsLife · Birth – 5th Grade

We want your kids to love church.

Follow Jesus. Grow in freedom. Share Christ with everyone.

Safe, fun, and full of Jesus.

We're committed to creating safe and fun environments where your children learn about the Bible and the love of Jesus through relevant, age-appropriate kids services. Our ultimate hope is to see a generation of children come to know God at an early age.

Fun

Energetic worship music, interactive games, and bright, friendly environments. Our team feels called to share the love of Jesus with kids and brings excitement to every Sunday.

Safety

Every staff member and volunteer has completed a background check. Our whole team is trained and immersed in a culture of safety and cleanliness with your child's wellbeing in mind.

Learning

From praying over infants to sharing relevant Bible lessons with elementary kids, our goal is for every child to learn about the Bible and experience God's presence weekly. KidsLife uses Orange Curriculum.

Commonly Asked Questions

For parents

What if it's our first time at KidsLife?

At KidsLife Check-In, our hosts will have you fill out a short form about your child (or children) and give you a Security Tag. If we need to reach you during the service, we'll send a text message to your phone. Present your Security Tag at the end of service to pick up your child. For safety, no child will be released without the correct Security Tag.

Can I come to KidsLife with my child?

You're welcome to peek in and see how your child is doing. To be in a classroom, we require volunteers to have a background check and interview with us, because the safety of your child is of the utmost importance. If you'd like to serve in a class, we'd love to have you on the team! Email us to find out more.

What will my child do?

Your child will have a blast! Every classroom has activities and toys designed for your child's age. They'll experience praise and worship, a creatively communicated Bible lesson, crafts, games, and more. We have fun serving God, and it's contagious!

What class does my child go to?

KidsLife has three main groups: Little Life (birth – 1 year), KidsLife Jr. (2 years – preschool), and KidsLife (K – 5th grade). Have other questions? Email us.

How can I lead my child to Christ?

One of our chief goals is to equip KidsLife parents to lead their children to know God from an early age. When your child begins asking faith questions, God may be opening their heart to the Gospel. View our parent guide so you're ready when that moment comes.
