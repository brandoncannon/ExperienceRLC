RLC Connect

Your next step starts here.

Second Sunday of every month, with one goal: give you what you need to connect with a church community and take your next steps with Jesus. Lunch is free and childcare is provided.

What you'll hear about

One lunch. Five conversations.

1

Our Story & Vision

The behind-the-scenes story of RLC through the journey of our pastors from Alabama to Bowling Green, where we are today, and where we're headed. We exist to develop fully devoted followers of Jesus Christ by helping people Know God, Find Freedom, Discover their God-given Purpose, and Make a Difference.

2

Community

We're not a church who has LifeGroups; we're a church of LifeGroups, where you can connect, protect, and grow together.

3

Serving

You're never closer to the heart of God than when you're serving together to make a difference. Discover which team best fits your gifts and passions.

4

Ownership

We don't exist for the church; we are the church, and we exist to reach the world. Move from spiritual consumer to spiritual contributor.

5

Next Steps

Our hope is that you become a fully devoted follower of Jesus, keep taking next steps, and help others around you do the same.
