Thankful in the Middle

Pastor Brandon Cannon · November 15, 2026

Watch this message
0 of 5 blanks

The Big Idea

Gratitude isn't a feeling I wait for. It's a choice I make.

Most of us are thankful once things work out. Paul wrote Philippians from a prison cell, and it's the most joyful letter he ever wrote. He didn't wait for the ending to say thank you.

Philippians 4:6-7 NLT

6Don’t worry about anything; instead, pray about everything. Tell God what you need, and thank him for all he has done. 7Then you will experience God’s peace, which exceeds anything we can understand. His peace will guard your hearts and minds as you live in Christ Jesus.

1Gratitude remembers what God has already done.

Luke 17:15-16 NLT

15One of them, when he saw that he was healed, came back to Jesus, shouting, “Praise God!” 16He fell to the ground at Jesus’ feet, thanking him for what he had done. This man was a Samaritan.

Ten men were healed. Only one came back. The other nine weren't bad people. They were just in a hurry to get on with their lives.

2Gratitude refuses to let my circumstances have the last word.

Philippians 4:11-13 NLT

11Not that I was ever in need, for I have learned how to be content with whatever I have. 12I know how to live on almost nothing or with everything. I have learned the secret of living in every situation, whether it is with a full stomach or empty, with plenty or little. 13For I can do everything through Christ, who gives me strength.

Paul says he learned to be content. That means it can be learned. Nobody is born good at this.

  • Content doesn't mean complacent.
  • Content means my peace isn't for sale.

3Gratitude turns my worry into worship.

1 Thessalonians 5:16-18 NLT

16Always be joyful. 17Never stop praying. 18Be thankful in all circumstances, for this is God’s will for you who belong to Christ Jesus.

The verse says in all circumstances, not for all circumstances. You don't have to be thankful for the diagnosis. You can be thankful in it.

This week

The Thank-You Week: each day, text one person and tell them one specific reason you're thankful for them. Check off each day you do it.

For LifeGroups & familyTalk It Over4 questions
  1. When is a time you only saw God's faithfulness looking back?
  2. Which comes easier for you: remembering or worrying? Why do you think that is?
  3. What circumstance do you need to stop letting have the last word?
  4. Read Philippians 4:6-7 out loud together. What do you need to tell God this week?
